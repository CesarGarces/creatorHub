# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Creator Hub ("we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform and use our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Personal Information Provided by You

- **Account Data:** Name, email address, and hashed credentials provided during registration.
- **Creator Style & RAG Context:** User-submitted brand guidelines, preferences, past scripts, or style context uploaded to personalize AI outputs.
- **User Content:** Prompts, scripts, images, thumbnails, and other content created or uploaded within the platform.
- **Payment & Transaction Data:** Subscription plans, credit purchase history, and credit balance. Payment processing is handled directly by third-party processors (e.g., Stripe, Mercado Pago); we do not store full payment card details.

### Automatically Collected Information

- IP address, browser type, device details, and operating system.
- Usage analytics, feature engagement, pages visited, and referral sources.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- Provide, maintain, and personalize our AI-powered creator tools and services.
- Process transactions, manage credit balances, and send billing receipts.
- Manage community bot integrations and user workflow automation.
- Improve platform functionality, security, and performance through analytics.
- Send administrative updates, security alerts, and customer support responses.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Sharing Your Information & Third-Party Vendors

We do not sell your personal data. We share necessary data only with trusted service providers:

- **AI Model Providers:** Prompts and input assets are processed via secure API endpoints (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini, and image synthesis services). Data processed via these API services is not used to train third-party foundation models.
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe and Mercado Pago for secure transaction handling.
- **Hosting & Infrastructure:** Cloud hosting (e.g., Vercel) and database providers.
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by applicable law or enforceable governmental request.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information, credit usage history, and AI outputs as long as your account remains active. You may request full account and data deletion at any time by contacting support. Upon account termination, personal data and associated style context will be permanently erased or anonymized, subject to retention required by tax or accounting laws.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

- Access, download, or transfer your personal data and generated content.
- Rectify inaccurate personal information.
- Request erasure of your account and creator context ("Right to be forgotten").
- Restrict or object to certain data processing activities.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@creatorhubplatform.com**.

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and local storage to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. For detailed information, please review our Cookie Policy.

## 8. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including encryption at rest and in transit, to protect your personal data and generated content.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in servers outside your country of residence. We ensure all cross-border data transfers comply with applicable privacy regulations through standard contractual clauses.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Creator Hub is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via platform notifications or email prior to taking effect.
