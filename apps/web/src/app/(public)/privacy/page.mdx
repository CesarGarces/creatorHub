## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Registration and Account Data
- **Identification data:** Full name, email address
- **Access credentials:** Password hash (we never store passwords in plain text)
- **Billing data:** Name, address, tax ID, payment data (processed by Stripe/Mercado Pago - see Section 5)

### 2.2 Usage Data and Creative Context (RAG)
To improve AI generation quality and personalize your experience:
- **Creative style and habits:** Frequent topics, preferred formats, tone of voice, publishing patterns
- **Generation history:** Prompts used, parameters selected, generated results
- **Platform interactions:** Tools used, frequency of use, configuration preferences

### 2.3 Generated and Uploaded Content
- **Images:** Uploaded by you or generated via AI (thumbnails, illustrations, etc.)
- **Scripts and texts:** Uploaded by you or generated via AI (video scripts, posts, etc.)
- **Associated metadata:** Dates, tools used, generation parameters

### 2.4 Technical and Navigation Data
- **IP address** (anonymized after 30 days)
- **Device type, browser, operating system**
- **Access and error logs** (for security and debugging)
- **Cookies and similar technologies** (see Cookie Policy)

### 2.5 Third-Party Data (Integrations)
When connecting external accounts:
- **YouTube/Twitch/OTHER PLATFORMS:** Access token (OAuth), channel ID, public statistics
- **Community data:** Chat messages, bot interactions (only if you explicitly authorize)

## 7. Data Security

- **Encryption in transit:** TLS 1.3 for all communications
- **Encryption at rest:** AES-256 for databases and file storage
- **Authentication:** bcrypt/argon2 for passwords, optional 2FA (TOTP)
- **Access:** Least privilege principle, audit logs, periodic reviews
- **Backups:** Encrypted, geo-redundant, 30-day retention
- **Target certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II (in progress)

## 10. Artificial Intelligence and Your Data

### 10.1 Third-Party Model Usage
We use **OpenAI, Google (Gemini), Anthropic** and other provider APIs to generate content.

**Important:** Your prompts, uploaded images, and creative context **are sent to these providers** to generate responses. Review their privacy policies:
- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- [Google AI Privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Anthropic Privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)

### 10.2 Model Training
**We do not use your data to train or improve AI models** (neither ours nor third parties'), unless you give explicit, separate consent for this.

### 10.3 RAG and Local Context
Your creative style and history are processed **locally on our infrastructure** (vector database) to improve responses. Not shared with third parties for this purpose.

## 11. Payments and Financial Data

Payments are processed exclusively through **Stripe** and **Mercado Pago**.

- **We DO NOT store** full card numbers, CVV, or complete banking credentials.

- Stripe and Mercado Pago are independent controllers of payment data.
- See their policies: [Stripe Privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy) | [Mercado Pago Privacy](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/privacidad)
