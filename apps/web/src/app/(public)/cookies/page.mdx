# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. In addition to cookies, Creator Hub ("we," "our," or "us") utilizes standard browser storage technologies, such as **Local Storage** and **Session Storage** (collectively, "Storage Technologies"), to ensure seamless service functionality, authentication, and user preferences.

## 2. How We Use Storage Technologies

We use cookies and browser storage for the following purposes:

- **Essential & Security:** Required for core service operations, user authentication, session persistence, and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection.
- **Preferences & State:** Remember your UI settings, theme (light/dark mode), credit balance display preferences, and temporary script/editor drafts.
- **Analytics & Performance:** Help us understand user traffic, application latency, and platform performance to improve features (e.g., Vercel Analytics, Google Analytics).
- **Payment & Fraud Prevention:** Placed by payment processors during checkout to ensure secure transaction processing and prevent fraudulent chargebacks.

## 3. Storage Technologies Used by Creator Hub

| Category           | Storage Method / Name            | Purpose                                                          | Duration               |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Authentication** | Session / Auth Token             | Keeps you securely logged in and manages active API requests     | Session / 30 Days      |
| **Security**       | CSRF Token                       | Protects against unauthorized cross-site request forgery         | Session                |
| **Preferences**    | `theme`, `creator_settings`      | Remembers your UI preferences and workspace layout               | 1 Year (Local Storage) |
| **Analytics**      | Vercel Analytics                 | Measures page responsiveness, bundle performance, and error logs | 1 Year                 |
| **Analytics**      | Google Analytics (`_ga`, `_gid`) | Tracks usage metrics, page views, and feature adoption           | Up to 2 Years          |
| **Payments**       | Stripe / Payment Gateway Cookies | Facilitates secure credit purchases and fraud detection          | Session / 1 Year       |

## 4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party service providers that may place cookies or access browser storage when you interact with Creator Hub:

- **Vercel Analytics:** Performance and reliability monitoring.
- **Google Analytics:** Traffic analysis and aggregated user flow insights.
- **Payment Gateways (Stripe / Mercado Pago):** Fraud prevention and secure checkout processing.

## 5. Your Cookie Choices & Controls

- **Browser Settings:** Most web browsers allow you to manage, block, or delete cookies and cleared Local Storage via your browser preferences/settings.
- **Essential Storage:** Please note that disabling essential cookies or local storage may prevent you from logging in or using Creator Hub features.
- **Analytics Opt-Out:** You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by visiting [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 6. Cookie Consent & Banner

When you first access Creator Hub, a cookie banner will notify you about our use of Storage Technologies. Essential cookies are loaded automatically to enable core platform operation. By continuing to use our platform or accepting non-essential cookies through the banner, you consent to our use of these technologies as outlined in this policy.

## 7. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" setting. Because uniform standards for DNT compliance have not been broadly established across the industry, Creator Hub does not currently respond to DNT signals.

## 8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect technical, operational, or legal changes. We will notify users of any material updates by posting the revised version with an updated "Last updated" date.
