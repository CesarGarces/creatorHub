# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** October 11, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how **Creator Hub** ("we", "our", "the Platform") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website and use our SaaS platform.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device, remember preferences, and improve your experience.

**Similar technologies** include: local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, web beacons, and fingerprinting techniques. This policy covers all of them.

---

## 2. Cookie Categories We Use

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary (Essential)
**Always active - cannot be disabled.** Required for the Platform to function.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| `session_id` / `__session` | Maintain login session, authenticate requests | Session / 30 days | First-party |
| `csrf_token` | CSRF protection on forms | Session | First-party |
| `auth_token` | Secure authentication (JWT in httpOnly cookie) | 15 min (access) / 30 days (refresh) | First-party |
| `cookie_consent` | Remember your cookie preferences | 1 year | First-party |
| `idempotency_key` | Prevent duplicate submissions (registration, payments) | Per request | First-party |

**Legal basis:** Contract performance (GDPR Art. 6.1.b) / Legitimate interest (security)

### 2.2 Preferences & Functionality
**Optional - require your consent.** Remember choices and enhance UX.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| `theme` | Dark/light/system theme preference | 1 year | First-party |
| `language` | Preferred language/locale | 1 year | First-party |
| `sidebar_state` | Collapsed/expanded sidebar state | Session | First-party |
| `onboarding_completed` | Track onboarding progress | 1 year | First-party |
| `tool_preferences` | Default tool settings, favorites | 1 year | First-party |

**Legal basis:** Consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a)

### 2.3 Analytics & Performance
**Optional - require your consent.** Understand usage to improve the Platform.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| `ph_session` / `ph_event` | PostHog: session replay, funnels, feature usage | 1 year | PostHog (EU hosting) |
| `_ga` / `_ga_*` | Google Analytics: traffic, conversions (if enabled) | 2 years | Google |
| `plausible_session` | Plausible: lightweight privacy-friendly analytics | 30 min | Plausible (EU) |

**Data collected:** Page views, clicks, feature usage, errors, performance metrics. **IP anonymized.** No PII.

**Legal basis:** Consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a / ePrivacy Art. 5.3)

### 2.4 Marketing & Personalization
**Optional - require your consent.** Relevant communications and onboarding.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| `intercom-session-*` | Intercom: live chat, in-app messages, onboarding tours | 1 week | Intercom |
| `customerio_*` | Customer.io: email automation, lifecycle campaigns | 1 year | Customer.io |
| `utm_*` / `ref_*` | Attribution: campaign source, referral tracking | 30 days | First-party |

**Legal basis:** Consent (GDPR Art. 6.1.a / ePrivacy Art. 5.3)

### 2.5 Third-Party / Embedded Content
**Set by external services when you interact with them.**

| Service | Purpose | Cookies |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Stripe / Mercado Pago** | Payment checkout, fraud prevention (3D Secure) | `__stripe_mid`, `__stripe_sid`, `mpsession` |
| **YouTube / Twitch / Discord** | OAuth login, embedded players, widgets | `VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE`, `YSC`, `td` |
| **OpenAI / Google / Anthropic** | API calls from our backend (no client cookies) | None client-side |

## 3. Cookie Consent Management

### 3.1 Consent Banner
On first visit, a **cookie banner** appears allowing you to:
- **Accept all** (all categories)
- **Reject non-essential** (only strictly necessary)
- **Customize** (granular per-category toggle)

### 3.2 Withdrawing/Changing Consent
You can change preferences anytime via:
- **Cookie icon** (bottom-left corner of Platform)
- **Settings → Privacy → Cookies** (when logged in)
- **Clear site data** in browser settings

### 3.3 Consent Records
We log: timestamp, IP (anonymized), user agent, categories accepted/rejected, banner version. Retained **1 year** for compliance evidence.

## 4. How to Control Cookies in Your Browser

### 4.1 Browser Settings
All major browsers let you:
- View/delete cookies for specific sites
- Block third-party cookies
- Block all cookies (may break functionality)
- Clear on exit

**Guides:**
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

### 4.2 Opt-Out Tools
- **Network Advertising Initiative:** optout.networkadvertising.org
- **Digital Advertising Alliance:** optout.aboutads.info
- **EU Your Online Choices:** youronlinechoices.eu

### 4.3 Do Not Track (DNT)
We respect `DNT: 1` header as a signal to disable analytics/marketing cookies automatically.

## 5. Specific Technologies Detail

### 5.1 Local Storage / Session Storage
Used for:
- **Client-side state:** UI preferences, draft content, tool inputs
- **Offline support:** Service worker cache for PWA
- **No expiry** (until cleared) for localStorage; sessionStorage clears on tab close

### 5.2 Service Workers / PWA
If you install Creator Hub as PWA:
- **Cache API:** Static assets for offline use
- **Background sync:** Queue mutations when offline
- **Push notifications:** Only with explicit permission

### 5.3 Tracking Pixels / Web Beacons
Used in **transactional emails** (invoices, security alerts) to track delivery/open rates. **Not used in marketing emails without consent.**
